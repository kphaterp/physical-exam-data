Bedside shortcut (McGee)

For pretest probabilities between ~10% and 90%, an LR shifts probability by roughly:

LRChange in probability
10+45%
5+30%
2+15%
1no change
0.5−15%
0.2−30%
0.1−45%

Remember 2-5-10 ↔ 15-30-45 (and the reciprocals 0.5-0.2-0.1).

Definitions

  • LR+ = sens / (1 − spec): how much a present finding raises odds.
  • LR− = (1 − sens) / spec: how much an absent finding lowers odds.
  • SpPIn / SnNOut: highly specific findings rule in when positive; highly sensitive findings rule out when negative.
  • Post-test odds = pretest odds × LR. Odds = p / (1 − p).
